# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of DSU Next, want this project to be a place where everyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to Android, DSU or open source.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and viewpoints.
- Giving feedback that is specific and useful, and accepting it with grace.
- Owning your mistakes, apologizing to the people affected and learning from them.
- Thinking about what is best for the community as a whole, not only for yourself.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home address or email address, without their permission.
- Demanding support, fixes or features in a hostile or entitled way. This is a free project maintained in spare time.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

DSU Next is a tool that can leave a device unable to boot. Questions about how that happened are welcome, blaming other users for it is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies everywhere the project is discussed: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code review comments and any other space that is part of DSU Next. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example by speaking for it on social media or at an event.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [Ditzzy (OhMyDitzzy)](https://github.com/OhMyDitzzy), through the contact options on that profile. Please include links or screenshots when you can. Do not open a public issue for a report.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported an incident, and will not share the report with anyone who does not need to know about it.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers will follow these steps when deciding the consequences of a violation of this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** Inappropriate language or behavior that was unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning that explains what was wrong and why. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including with those enforcing this Code of Conduct, for a set period. This includes avoiding interaction in project spaces and on external channels such as social media. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any kind of interaction or public communication with the community for a set period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this time. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact:** A pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.
